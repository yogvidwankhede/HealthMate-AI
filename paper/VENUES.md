## Update 2026-10-04: rules read for the BioNLP target (2026 call plus ACL Rolling Review rules)
- BioNLP 2026: full paper 8 pages of text plus unlimited references; short paper 4 pages; anonymous submission; ACL
  format. Deadline reported as 17 Apr 2026 in one source and 20 Apr 2026 in another (sources disagree; check the official page).
  The 2027 call was not yet published when I looked. No preprint, AI or checklist rules were stated on the pages I read.
- ACL Rolling Review rules (aclrollingreview.org/cfp): 8 / 4 pages plus unlimited references, limitations and ethics;
  a missing Limitations section means desk rejection; the Responsible NLP checklist is mandatory and misleading answers
  can mean desk rejection; ACL removed anonymity periods in Feb 2024 (a binding "no non-anonymous preprint" option exists);
  generative-AI help for language polishing, literature search and low-novelty text is allowed with disclosure, and authors
  are fully responsible for correctness.
- Fit: current draft is 6 pages including tables, a Limitations section before the references, and a disclosure paragraph.
  Checklist draft: paper/RESPONSIBLE_NLP_CHECKLIST.md.
